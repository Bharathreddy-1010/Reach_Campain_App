# Reach — Product Context

## Vision & Purpose
**Reach** is a premium mobile platform for community-funded message amplification. It empowers individuals, independent researchers, educators, non-profits, civic organizers, and mission-driven initiatives to expand the public reach of important messages through collective funding of advertising and distribution campaigns.

## Core Non-Negotiable Business Rules
1. **Distribution, Not Direct Donation:**
   Every contribution directly purchases verified ad impressions, distribution reach, and platform delivery services. Contributions are **never** personal donations or unrestricted charitable gifts.
2. **Deterministic & Transparent Reach Model:**
   Reach is modeled predictably (default demo baseline: ₹100 = 1,000 impressions / ~750 unique individuals reached). These are explicitly presented as calculated baseline estimates based on historical platform averages, never as guaranteed commercial outcomes.
3. **Mandatory Editorial Review:**
   Funding completion does **not** automatically trigger advertising. Campaigns must clear platform review, source verification, and ad creative compliance before entering the distribution queue.
4. **Transparent Fee & Allocation Structure:**
   - 85% Direct Ad Spend & Media Placements
   - 10% Campaign Review, Ad Operations & Compliance
   - 5% Payment Processing & Administrative Security
5. **Unified Role:**
   A single user account can discover, read, back, create, and monitor campaigns seamlessly without artificial role segregation.

## Primary Navigation Architecture
- **Home:** Hero proposition, reach model explanation, featured campaigns, live amplification feed, "How It Works" (Create → Review → Amplify).
- **Explore:** Category filtering (Environment, Health, Science, Education, Public Policy, Economic Policy, Community, Arts & Culture, Business, Other), full-text search, sorting (Trending, Newest, Most Funded, Reach Target).
- **Create:** 5-step sequential creation engine:
  1. Basics (Title with 60-char counter, category, creator identity, purpose, summary)
  2. Story (Editorial composer, narrative sections, images, data callouts, verified sources, audience actions)
  3. Visuals & Ads (Cover image, 1:1 and 9:16 ad visual mockups, ad headlines, CTA choices, placement preview)
  4. Reach (Geographic focus, target audience characteristics, channel selection, reach calculations)
  5. Submit (Complete review, explicit creator-defined funding goal, policy checkboxes, submission confirmation)
- **Activity:** In-app notification center tracking milestones, approvals, contribution receipts, and campaign delivery reports.
- **Profile & Creator Dashboard:** Single account managing backed campaigns, contribution receipts, bookmarks, and creator campaign lifecycle controls.
